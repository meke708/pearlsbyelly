Our Story

Pearls By Elly began with a simple passion for creating beautiful, meaningful accessories right here in Ongwediva, Namibia. What started as a hobby has grown into a small boutique dedicated to handmade excellence.

We specialize in a variety of styles, including beaded, charm, friendship, and gemstone bracelets. Our heart lies in creating personalized pieces that speak to the individual wearing them.

Every bracelet is carefully crafted by hand, ensuring that no two pieces are exactly alike—just like the women and girls who wear them.
